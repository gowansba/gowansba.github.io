Case study — Email creative

Designing for what comes after the goal.

The problem: most post-purchase email speaks to the goal a customer already hit — not what happens once they get there. A retention-stage campaign needed a tone shift: less "start here," more "protect what you've built."

The approach: translated symptom- and benefit-based product copy into an editorial, almost macro-photography visual direction — muted forest greens, serif italic headlines, restrained typography — instead of the bright, high-energy look most supplement email defaults to.

The execution: built the template hand-coded in HTML rather than a drag-and-drop builder, with a full-bleed hero, a quiet mid-page storytelling block, and a closing CTA panel that mirrors the hero's tone.

  • Role: creative direction, copy, and HTML build
  • Result: became the reference template for the retention-stage series that followed

Case study — Integrated campaign

Turning a hashtag into a household habit.

The brief: design a community challenge that drives daily engagement across Instagram and Facebook for eight straight weeks — not a one-off giveaway.

The mechanic: a simple daily framework (Fitness, Intake, Track) families could actually sustain, paired with a downloadable guide, a printable goal sheet, and a two-month tracking calendar to keep the habit visible at home, not just online.

The incentive structure: weekly prize drawings kept short-term motivation high, while a cumulative entry system built toward a grand-prize trip — rewarding both quick wins and sustained participation.

  • Role: campaign design, mechanics, and creative assets (guide, tracker, social rules)
  • Channels: Instagram, Facebook, owned email/SMS

Week 1

Launch. Comment-to-join mechanic drives guide downloads and follow growth.

Monday drawings. A new winner announced every week to sustain momentum.

8 weeks

Daily tracking. Printable calendar keeps the habit going offline, not just in-feed.

Finale

Grand prize. Entry totals over the full window decide the top winners.

Case study — Marketing systems

Retired the drag-and-drop builder. Wrote the emails in code instead.

Inherited a lifecycle program built on a template drag-and-drop tool and a legacy SMS platform. Rebuilt both the tooling and the workflow:

  • Platform migration: moved SMS and email infrastructure from Ziplingo to Attentive, rebuilding segmentation and automations from scratch.
  • Code-based builds: shifted the team off drag-and-drop email templates to hand-coded HTML, giving full control over rendering, dark mode, and mobile behavior across clients.
  • AI-assisted workflow: used Claude Code and ChatGPT to accelerate template builds, extract and QA shop links across US/CA campaign variants, and audit creator content against brand claims guidelines.
  • Custom reporting: built an AI-generated performance dashboard that pulls campaign and revenue data into a leadership-ready readout, replacing manual monthly reporting.
